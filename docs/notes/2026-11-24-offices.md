# Insurance, estate agent, tax and financial advisor offices

No matcher took `office=*`, so OSMDiffs didn't conflate any AllThePlaces (ATP) offices
([brawer/osmdiffs#1018](https://github.com/brawer/osmdiffs/issues/1018)). These four kinds account for most of the
offices in the ATP run of 2026-11-14:

| kind | ATP features | spiders | OSM objects (taginfo) | largest spiders |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `office=insurance` | 57,620 | 32 | 81,160 | `state_farm_us` 11,401, `farmers_insurance_us` 9,319, `axa_fr` 8,824, `allianz_de` 6,592 |
| `office=estate_agent` | 10,589 | 27 | 103,695 | `coldwell_banker_us` 1,805, `orpi` 1,212, `foncia_fr` 844 |
| `office=financial_advisor` | 10,383 | 6 | 8,728 | `edward_jones` 6,205, `ameriprise_us` 3,382 |
| `office=tax_advisor` | 9,631 | 3 | 14,279 | `jackson_hewitt` 5,006, `vlh_de` 3,040, `liberty_tax_us` 1,585 |

## The change

These offices work like shops: each is a branded place at a street address, and most have their own web page. So the
four values get the `SHOP` mask in `MatchMask::add_tag`, and the POI matcher handles them like any shop: a 400 m
radius and the same rules (`brand_wikidata`, `contact`, `brand_name`, `brand_in_name`,
`brand_domain`, `address`). They also get a new modifier bit, `UNIMPORTANT`, which caps their importance by kind at 0.5,
below shops (0.7) and fuel stations (0.6), since they matter less to map users. Other `office` values still have no
mask. `office=employment_agency` (5,011 features)
could be next.

The issue raised two concerns:

- **Agencies named after the agent**, such as Edward Jones' `Jeff Barney` or Farmers' `David B Rasmussen - 7148 W
  Addison St`. All of the large spiders set `brand:wikidata`, and so do many OSM offices, so most matches are by
  brand. The agent's name only comes into play for the `address` rule, where the OSM name has to fit the ATP name.
- **Jackson Hewitt counters in Walmart.** The two have different `brand:wikidata` values, so a counter can't match
  its Walmart. It can only become `located_in` if the spider sets `located_in`, and today it doesn't, although about
  half of its office pages say "Located in Walmart"
  ([alltheplaces#20798](https://github.com/alltheplaces/alltheplaces/issues/20798)).

### Offices without a street address make no creates

`axa_fr` lists 4,389 "AXA Épargne et Protection" advisors who meet customers at home, at work or by video. They have
no street address, and the spider puts them at the town center
([alltheplaces#20800](https://github.com/alltheplaces/alltheplaces/issues/20800)). Other checks, mostly
the one for positions that several features share, already keep 1,065 of them out, but the other 3,324 would have
become creates in France. So an ATP office with
no `addr:street_address`, `addr:street` or `addr:full` never becomes a create (`excluded_kind`, reason
`office_without_address`). This holds for every `office` value, since an office that can't give its address is
rarely one that people visit. AXA agencies that have an address are not affected.

## Results in the eval regions

Each run used ATP of 2026-11-14, filtered by `addr:country`, and ran the full pipeline with osmdiffs at ab64b0b against
this change. The extracts were Berlin and Massachusetts from the [eval regions](2026-11-06-eval-regions.md),
Switzerland, and Île-de-France (openstreetmap.fr, 2026-11-21), which I added because the French spiders are among
the largest. Fallbacks for broken spiders were turned off, since a country split looks like a run where most spiders
broke. Only ATP features inside the region's polygon count.

| region | ATP offices | `same` | `missing_in_osm` | deletion candidates | creates | modifies |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Île-de-France | 2,898 | 1,285 (44%) | 1,613 | 196 | 813 | 497 |
| Germany: Berlin | 348 | 103 (30%) | 245 | 13 | 93 | 55 |
| US: Massachusetts | 402 | 34 (8%) | 368 | 6 | 0 | 37 |
| Switzerland | 2 | 0 | 2 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

On main, every column is 0, because the offices had no rows. ATP has almost no Swiss offices of these kinds: only 2
from `jll`. In Massachusetts, none of the office spiders passed the create gate, which needs 20 matched features.
State Farm has no agents in Massachusetts.

Île-de-France had 1,072 office creates before the address rule and 813 after. All 259 that it leaves out are
`axa_fr` advisors. Across France, `axa_fr` creates went from 7,373 to 4,053.

By rule, among the `same` rows:

| region | `brand_wikidata` | `contact` | `brand_in_name` | `brand_name` | `address` | `brand_domain` |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Île-de-France | 1,167 | 73 | 39 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
| Berlin | 98 | 1 | 3 | 0 | 1 | 0 |
| Massachusetts | 33 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

For `brand_wikidata` matches, the median distance is 8 m in Île-de-France, 9 m in Berlin and 19 m in Massachusetts,
and the 90th percentile is 33 m, 38 m and 203 m.

### Precision

This follows the phone check in [CONFLATED_PARQUET.md](../outputs/CONFLATED_PARQUET.md#estimating-the-precision-of-a-rule):
among rows where both sides have a phone number, the share where the numbers agree.

| region | `brand_wikidata` | `brand_in_name` | `brand_name` | `brand_domain` |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Île-de-France | 93% of 1,046 | 89% of 63 | 3 of 3 | 7 of 7 |
| Berlin | 65% of 40 | 1 of 1 | | |
| Massachusetts | 6 of 6 | | | |

Berlin's low share comes from Allianz agents. ATP gives the agent's own number, often a mobile, and OSM gives the
office line. Of the 14 Berlin matches where the phones differ, 10 have the same street address on both sides, and the
other 4 have no OSM address. In Île-de-France, 59 of the 71 matches with differing phones have no OSM address.

The wrong matches I found:

- `Human Immobilier` matched `Bourse de l'Immobilier` by `address`, because `immobilier` counts as a distinctive
  name word. It is one of only 2 `address` matches there.
- 5 AXA advisors without an address matched AXA agencies 62–356 m away by `brand_wikidata`. In 2 of them, the phone
  and website agree, so the OSM node is the advisor.

44 Allianz rows in Berlin share 17 OSM offices, and 130 rows in Île-de-France share 59 OSM offices. These are several
agents in one office, which the matcher allows within 25 m (`COLOCATED_METERS`).

### Deletion candidates

Unmatched OSM offices of a brand that ATP covers become deletion candidates. In Île-de-France there are 196: Orpi
59, Laforêt 37, Guy Hoquet 28, Foncia 14, Allianz 11, AXA 8 and others. Their mean `local_share` is 0.80. 14 of the
196 share a phone number or website with an ATP office of the same brand within 50 m, which matched another OSM
office instead. In those cases, OSM maps two offices of one agency, often its sales and its property management,
while ATP lists one. The matcher took the closer OSM office rather than the one whose phone agrees. In Berlin, all 13
candidates are Allianz agencies. One of them, Benjamin Kirsch's, is in ATP at another address (Köpenicker Straße
121a, `missing_in_osm`), so the agent has moved.

### Other rows

- Berlin: the `deutsche_bank_de` "Deutsche Bank Private Banking Center" (`amenity=bank`) now matches the OSM
  `office=financial_advisor` of the same brand, so its create became a modify. This is correct.
- Île-de-France: 20 creates of banks and opticians are now held back as `similar_nearby`. The reason is an OSM office
  within 100 m whose name shares a word with theirs. That word is the town, from the office's `official_name`:
  `Orpi Anma Gestion Immo Crosne` holds back a BNP Paribas in Crosne. This flaw in `similar.rs` predates this
  change, since place names count as distinctive words. Offices just make it show more often, because French
  agencies put the town in `official_name`.
- No other row changed in any region.

## Follow-ups

- [#1049](https://github.com/brawer/osmdiffs/issues/1049): when several OSM features share an ATP feature's
  `brand:wikidata`, prefer the one whose phone or website agrees over the closer one. That would remove the 14 wrong
  deletion candidates described above.
- [#1050](https://github.com/brawer/osmdiffs/issues/1050): in `similar.rs`, leave place names out of the distinctive
  name words.
- [#1051](https://github.com/brawer/osmdiffs/issues/1051): `office=employment_agency`.
